# Contract K001

**Data Processing Agreement** between ShipRaftaar Logistics Pvt Ltd (the "Company", Data Fiduciary) and RouteMind Technologies Pvt Ltd (the "Vendor", Data Processor).

**1. Scope of Services**

Services. RouteMind provides delivery-route optimisation and shipment-tracking software under the Order Form.

**2. Details of Processing**

RouteMind processes the following under this Agreement — purpose: optimising delivery routes and sending shipment-tracking updates to the Company's customers; data: recipient names, delivery addresses, phone numbers and delivery-time records; data principals: recipients of the Company's shipments; retention period: the term of the Agreement plus no more than thirty (30) days.

**3. Instructions and Lawful Basis**

Instructions. RouteMind processes Personal Data solely on ShipRaftaar's documented instructions; ShipRaftaar warrants that each instructed processing has a lawful ground, including Data Principal consent where needed.

**4. Security and Breach Notification**

Security. RouteMind will protect Personal Data with appropriate technical and organisational measures. If RouteMind becomes aware of a Personal Data Breach it will tell ShipRaftaar without undue delay and within seventy-two (72) hours, and will give ShipRaftaar all details required for ShipRaftaar's notices to the Data Protection Board and to affected Data Principals.

**5. Sub-processors**

Sub-processors. Prior written approval from ShipRaftaar is required before RouteMind engages any sub-processor; each approved sub-processor must be bound by data-protection terms at least as protective as these.

**6. Data Location and Transfers**

Location. Data is stored and processed in Gurugram and Noida (India) only. RouteMind may transfer data abroad only with ShipRaftaar's prior written consent, appropriate safeguards, and never to a country restricted by the Central Government.

**7. Confidentiality**

Confidentiality. Each party must protect the other's confidential information and use it solely to perform this Agreement.

**8. Limitation of Liability**

Liability. Neither party's liability shall exceed twelve (12) months' fees, except that RouteMind's liability for any data-protection or confidentiality breach shall be the greater of three times those fees or ₹1,50,00,000 (one crore fifty lakh rupees).

**9. Indemnification**

Indemnity. RouteMind indemnifies ShipRaftaar against third-party claims resulting from RouteMind's breach of this Agreement.

**10. Return and Deletion of Data**

Exit. Within thirty (30) days after termination or expiry, RouteMind must return or delete all Personal Data at ShipRaftaar's option and certify the deletion in writing.

**11. Audit Rights**

Audit. ShipRaftaar (or an independent auditor it appoints) may audit RouteMind's data-processing practices annually and after any Personal Data Breach.

**12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

Governing law. The laws of India apply, and the courts in New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.
