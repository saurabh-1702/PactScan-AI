# Contract K016

**Data Processing Agreement** between TalentLoop HR Solutions Pvt Ltd (the "Company", Data Fiduciary) and PeopleCore Payroll Services Pvt Ltd (the "Vendor", Data Processor).

**1. Scope of Services**

PeopleCore will provide TalentLoop with payroll processing and employee-records management, as set out in the Order Form.

**2. Details of Processing**

Details of processing. Purpose: running payroll and maintaining employment records for the Company's clients. Personal data categories: employee names, PAN, bank details, salary and attendance records. Data Principals: employees of the Company's client organisations. Retention: for the duration of the services, and thereafter only until deletion under the termination clause (no more than thirty (30) days).

**3. Instructions and Lawful Basis**

PeopleCore acts only on TalentLoop's documented instructions. TalentLoop is responsible for having a lawful basis, including any consent required from Data Principals, for the processing it asks PeopleCore to carry out.

**4. Children's Personal Data**

PeopleCore does not expect to process data of children. If any child's personal data is to be processed, PeopleCore will process it only once TalentLoop has obtained verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian, and PeopleCore will not carry out tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising aimed at children.

**5. Security and Breach Notification**

PeopleCore shall maintain reasonable security safeguards (including encryption at rest and role-based access control) and shall inform TalentLoop of every personal data breach without undue delay, and no later than forty-eight (48) hours after discovering it, and shall assist TalentLoop in notifying the Board and the affected Data Principals.

**6. Sub-processors**

PeopleCore may use a sub-processor only after TalentLoop has approved it in writing in advance, and PeopleCore shall flow down to every sub-processor the same data-protection obligations it owes under this Agreement.

**7. Data Location and Transfers**

All personal data will be hosted in PeopleCore's facilities in Chennai and Coimbatore, India. Any transfer outside India requires TalentLoop's written consent, appropriate safeguards, and may only be to a jurisdiction not restricted by the Central Government.

**8. Confidentiality**

Both parties will keep the other's confidential information secret and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement.

**9. Limitation of Liability**

Save for breaches of data-protection or confidentiality obligations, PeopleCore's total liability is limited to the fees paid by TalentLoop in the preceding twelve (12) months. For those breaches, PeopleCore's liability shall be not less than the greater of twelve (12) months' fees and ₹4,00,00,000 (four crore rupees).

**10. Indemnification**

PeopleCore will indemnify TalentLoop against any third-party claim caused by PeopleCore's breach of this Agreement.

**11. Return and Deletion of Data**

Upon termination of this Agreement, the Vendor shall delete Personal Data within one hundred and eighty (180) days.

**12. Audit Rights**

TalentLoop may audit PeopleCore's processing of personal data, itself or through an independent auditor, at least once a year and also after any personal data breach.

**13. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

Indian law governs this Agreement; the courts at Chennai have exclusive jurisdiction.

**14. Schedule 2 — Amendment No. 1**

Notwithstanding Clause 11 (Return and Deletion of Data), the Vendor shall, at the Company's election, return or delete all Personal Data within thirty (30) days of termination and certify the deletion in writing.
