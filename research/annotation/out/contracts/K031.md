# Contract K031

**Data Processing Agreement** between PaySetu Fintech Pvt Ltd (the "Company", Data Fiduciary) and LedgerGrid Infrastructure Pvt Ltd (the "Vendor", Data Processor).

**1. Scope of Services**

The Vendor shall provide transaction-ledger hosting and fraud-monitoring infrastructure as described in the Order Form.

**2. Details of Processing**

Schedule 1 sets out the processing: purpose — hosting the Company's payment ledger and running fraud-monitoring rules on it; categories of personal data — names, bank account identifiers, transaction records and device identifiers; categories of Data Principals — the Company's account holders; retention — personal data is kept only for the term of this Agreement and deleted within thirty (30) days after it ends.

**3. Instructions and Lawful Basis**

The Vendor shall process Personal Data only on the documented instructions of the Company, and the Company confirms that it has a lawful ground, including the valid consent of Data Principals where required, for all processing it instructs.

**4. Security and Breach Notification**

The Vendor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect Personal Data, including encryption and access controls, and shall notify the Company of any Personal Data Breach without undue delay and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of it, providing the information the Company needs to inform the Data Protection Board of India and each affected Data Principal.

**5. Sub-processors**

The Vendor shall not engage any sub-processor without the Company's prior written approval and shall impose on each approved sub-processor data-protection obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement.

**6. Data Location and Transfers**

Personal Data shall be stored and processed only in data centres located in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai, India. The Vendor shall not transfer Personal Data outside India except to a country not restricted by the Central Government, with the Company's prior written consent and appropriate safeguards.

**7. Confidentiality**

Each party shall keep confidential all information disclosed by the other party and shall use it only to perform this Agreement.

**8. Limitation of Liability**

Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, provided that for breaches of data-protection or confidentiality obligations the Vendor's liability shall be the greater of three (3) times such fees or ₹10,00,00,000 (ten crore rupees).

**9. Indemnification**

The Vendor shall indemnify the Company against third-party claims arising from the Vendor's breach of this Agreement.

**10. Return and Deletion of Data**

Upon termination or expiry of this Agreement, the Vendor shall, at the Company's election, return or delete all Personal Data within thirty (30) days and shall certify the deletion in writing.

**11. Audit Rights**

The Company, directly or through an independent auditor, may audit the Vendor's data-processing practices once in every year and additionally following any Personal Data Breach.

**12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India and the courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
