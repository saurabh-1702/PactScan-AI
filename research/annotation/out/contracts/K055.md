# Contract K055

**Data Processing Agreement** between EduSpark Learning Pvt Ltd (the "Company", Data Fiduciary) and StackPeak Cloud Solutions Pvt Ltd (the "Vendor", Data Processor).

**1. Scope of Services**

Services. StackPeak provides hosting of the Company's online learning platform and its student-progress database under the Order Form.

**2. Instructions and Lawful Basis**

Instructions. StackPeak processes Personal Data solely on EduSpark's documented instructions; EduSpark warrants that each instructed processing has a lawful ground, including Data Principal consent where needed.

**3. Children's Personal Data**

Children's data. Student records of children are processed only after EduSpark obtains verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian. StackPeak shall not track, behaviourally monitor or direct targeted advertising at children.

**4. Security and Breach Notification**

Security. StackPeak will protect Personal Data with appropriate technical and organisational measures. If StackPeak becomes aware of a Personal Data Breach it will tell EduSpark without undue delay and within twenty-four (24) hours, and will give EduSpark all details required for EduSpark's notices to the Data Protection Board and to affected Data Principals.

**5. Sub-processors**

Sub-processors. Prior written approval from EduSpark is required before StackPeak engages any sub-processor; each approved sub-processor must be bound by data-protection terms at least as protective as these.

**6. Data Location and Transfers**

Location. Data is stored and processed in Bengaluru and Delhi NCR (India) only. StackPeak may transfer data abroad only with EduSpark's prior written consent, appropriate safeguards, and never to a country restricted by the Central Government.

**7. Confidentiality**

Confidentiality. Each party must protect the other's confidential information and use it solely to perform this Agreement.

**8. Limitation of Liability**

Liability. Neither party's liability shall exceed twelve (12) months' fees, except that StackPeak's liability for any data-protection or confidentiality breach shall be the greater of three times those fees or ₹3,00,00,000 (three crore rupees).

**9. Indemnification**

Indemnity. StackPeak indemnifies EduSpark against third-party claims resulting from StackPeak's breach of this Agreement.

**10. Return and Deletion of Data**

Exit. Within thirty (30) days after termination or expiry, StackPeak must return or delete all Personal Data at EduSpark's option and certify the deletion in writing.

**11. Audit Rights**

Audit. EduSpark (or an independent auditor it appoints) may audit StackPeak's data-processing practices annually and after any Personal Data Breach.

**12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

Governing law. The laws of India apply, and the courts in Bengaluru have exclusive jurisdiction.
