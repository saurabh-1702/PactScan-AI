# Contract K065

**Data Processing Agreement** between Kiranmart Retail Pvt Ltd (the "Company", Data Fiduciary) and DataNest Analytics LLP (the "Vendor", Data Processor).

**1. Scope of Services**

DataNest will provide Kiranmart with customer-analytics dashboards and loyalty-programme data processing, as set out in the Order Form.

**2. Details of Processing**

Details of processing. Purpose: operating the Company's loyalty programme and producing sales analytics for the Company. Personal data categories: names, mobile numbers, purchase history and loyalty-point balances. Data Principals: members of the Company's loyalty programme. Retention: for the duration of the services, and thereafter only until deletion under the termination clause (no more than thirty (30) days).

**3. Instructions and Lawful Basis**

DataNest acts only on Kiranmart's documented instructions. Kiranmart is responsible for having a lawful basis, including any consent required from Data Principals, for the processing it asks DataNest to carry out.

**4. Children's Personal Data**

DataNest does not expect to process data of children. If any child's personal data is to be processed, DataNest will process it only once Kiranmart has obtained verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian, and DataNest will not carry out tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising aimed at children.

**5. Security and Breach Notification**

DataNest shall maintain reasonable security safeguards (including encryption at rest and role-based access control) and shall inform Kiranmart of every personal data breach without undue delay, and no later than forty-eight (48) hours after discovering it, and shall assist Kiranmart in notifying the Board and the affected Data Principals.

**6. Sub-processors**

DataNest may use a sub-processor only after Kiranmart has approved it in writing in advance, and DataNest shall flow down to every sub-processor the same data-protection obligations it owes under this Agreement.

**7. Data Location and Transfers**

All personal data will be hosted in DataNest's facilities in Hyderabad and Pune, India. Any transfer outside India requires Kiranmart's written consent, appropriate safeguards, and may only be to a jurisdiction not restricted by the Central Government.

**8. Confidentiality**

Both parties will keep the other's confidential information secret and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement.

**9. Limitation of Liability**

DataNest's aggregate liability for all claims, including data-protection and confidentiality breaches, is limited to one (1) month's fees.

**10. Indemnification**

DataNest will indemnify Kiranmart against any third-party claim caused by DataNest's breach of this Agreement.

**11. Return and Deletion of Data**

When this Agreement ends, DataNest shall return or delete (as Kiranmart chooses) all personal data within thirty (30) days and give Kiranmart a written certificate of deletion.

**12. Audit Rights**

Kiranmart may audit DataNest's processing of personal data, itself or through an independent auditor, at least once a year and also after any personal data breach.

**13. Governing Law and Jurisdiction**

Indian law governs this Agreement; the courts at Hyderabad have exclusive jurisdiction.
